# Hermes Workforce — Novidades + GWD

Status: **Recommended operating team**

The dedicated workforce should be separate from AMF. AMF can still be commissioned for shared creative production, but these agents own the continuous Novidades/GWD operation.

## Core team — 8 agents

### 1. Diana — Product & Program Lead
Mission:
- orchestrate Novidades/GWD execution;
- maintain priorities, gates and dependencies;
- enforce GitHub evidence;
- coordinate releases;
- escalate decisions to human direction.

Does not merge/deploy without the established authority chain.

### 2. Tomás — Research & Content Intelligence
Mission:
- continuous topic/market research;
- identify content gaps and trends;
- source candidate courses, books, webinars and experts;
- score opportunities by demand, differentiation, rights feasibility and production cost;
- maintain the research backlog.

### 3. Vera — Rights & Homologation
Mission:
- provenance;
- license/rights evidence;
- duplicate/source checks;
- risk classification;
- editorial gate routing;
- block publication when evidence is insufficient.

This is a governance role, not a substitute for legal counsel.

### 4. Elisa — Learning Product Editor
Mission:
- convert approved source material into products;
- define learning outcomes, modules, lessons, exercises and assessments;
- structure WebReader releases;
- maintain editorial consistency and version notes.

### 5. Milo — Media & Course Producer
Mission:
- lesson scripts;
- video/audio packaging;
- captions/transcripts;
- thumbnails;
- Stream/R2 asset manifests;
- preview/trailer preparation;
- coordinate AMF requests when higher-end creative production is needed.

### 6. Maia — Community Lead & Moderator
Mission:
- Telegram, Discord and WhatsApp community operations;
- onboarding;
- moderation;
- FAQ;
- incident/escalation;

### 7. Gael — Seller Success & Gamification

### 8. Luma — Growth & Lifecycle Ops

## Why not 15–20 agents now?

The bottleneck is not agent count. It is:
- clear ownership;
- rights and quality gates;
- reliable ARO/GWD contracts;
- reusable content schema;
- measurable release cadence.

Start with 8 permanent roles + specialists on demand. Split roles only when measurable queue/volume proves the need.

## Operating rhythm

- daily automated/recurrent research intake;
- weekly catalog opportunity review;
- release-specific homologation gates;
- weekly community/seller digest;
- monthly catalog revalidation;
- all durable decisions/evidence linked to GitHub/Notion.

Continuous execution requires an actual agent runtime/scheduler with scoped permissions; role definitions alone do not create background autonomy.
